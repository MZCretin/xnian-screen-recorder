隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-09-12

中文

「XNian-屏幕录制」（以下简称"本应用"）尊重并保护你的隐私。我们不收集任何数据。

  • 本应用不联网：不包含任何网络请求、统计 SDK、广告 SDK 或崩溃上报
  • 你录制的视频和音频仅保存在你自己选择的本地文件夹，不会上传到任何服务器
  • 屏幕录制、麦克风、摄像头权限仅在你主动开始录制/开启画中画时使用，用途仅限生成你本人的录制文件
  • 本应用不收集、不存储、不共享任何个人信息、设备信息或使用数据
  • 本应用没有账号系统，无需注册

由于本应用不收集任何数据，因此不存在数据共享、数据出售或数据保留问题。如对本政策有疑问，请通过 App Store 页面的"技术支持"链接联系我们。

English

XNian Screen Recorder ("the app") respects and protects your privacy. We collect no data. Period.

  • The app makes no network connections: no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting SDKs
  • Your recordings are saved only to the local folder you choose; nothing is ever uploaded
  • Screen Recording, Microphone, and Camera permissions are used solely to produce your own recording files, and only while you are actively recording
  • The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information, device information, or usage data
  • There are no accounts and no sign-up

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or retain. Questions? Contact us via the Support link on the App Store page.
