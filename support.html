Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start recording?

The app lives in the menu bar (no Dock icon by default). Click the menu bar icon to open the main panel, choose Full Screen, Window, or Area, then click Start Recording. You can also use the global hotkey (default ⌥⌘R). Stop from the floating control bar, the menu bar icon, or the same hotkey.

macOS says it needs Screen Recording permission.

On first launch an onboarding window guides you to System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording and to enable XNian Screen Recorder there. macOS requires the app to relaunch after granting the permission — the onboarding window has a "Relaunch App" button for this.

Where are my recordings saved?

The first time you record, the app asks you to choose a folder using the standard macOS dialog. Your recordings are saved there, and the choice is remembered. You can change the folder any time in Preferences › General › Save to.

Can I record system sound and my microphone?

Yes. Enable system audio and/or the microphone in the main panel. They are mixed automatically, or you can write them as two separate tracks (Preferences › Audio) for easier editing. System audio capture requires macOS 13 or later.

How do I show my camera (picture-in-picture)?

Enable Camera overlay (picture-in-picture) in the main panel, or choose Show Camera Overlay from the menu bar icon. A draggable floating camera window appears and is included in the recording. Right-click it to change size, shape, or camera device. Camera permission is requested only when you first enable this.

What video formats and quality can I use?

Record to .mov or .mp4, up to 60 fps, with hardware-accelerated H.264 or HEVC, at native Retina resolution. Quality presets (high / medium / low / custom bitrate) are in Preferences › Video.

Does the app collect any data?

No. The app makes no network connections and collects no data. Your recordings stay on your Mac, only in the folder you choose. See our Privacy Policy.

常见问题（中文）

如何开始录制？

本应用常驻菜单栏（默认不显示 Dock 图标）。点击菜单栏图标打开主面板，选择全屏 / 窗口 / 区域，然后点开始录制；也可用全局快捷键（默认 ⌥⌘R）。停止录制可用悬浮控制条、菜单栏图标或同一快捷键。

提示需要"屏幕录制"权限怎么办？

首次启动会有引导窗口，引导你到系统设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 屏幕录制中勾选本应用。macOS 要求授权后重启 App，引导窗口内有"重新启动 App"按钮。

录制文件保存在哪里？

首次录制时，应用会通过 macOS 标准对话框让你选择一个保存文件夹，之后录制文件都存到该文件夹并记住你的选择。可随时在偏好设置 › 通用 › 保存位置中更改。

能同时录系统声音和麦克风吗？

可以。在主面板开启系统声音和/或麦克风，两者会自动混音；也可在偏好设置 › 音频中选择分为两条独立音轨，方便后期。系统声音录制需要 macOS 13 及以上。

如何开启摄像头画中画？

在主面板勾选摄像头悬浮窗（画中画），或从菜单栏图标选择显示摄像头悬浮窗。会出现一个可拖动的摄像头悬浮窗并录入画面，右键可切换大小、形状和设备。摄像头权限仅在首次开启时请求。

本应用会收集数据吗？

不会。应用不联网、不收集任何数据，录制文件仅保存在你自选的本地文件夹。详见隐私政策。
